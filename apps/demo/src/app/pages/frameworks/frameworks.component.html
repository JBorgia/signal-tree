One semantic model. Native to your framework.

SignalTree gives Angular, Vue, Solid and React one model for identity, causality, transactions, external truth and restoration — while each framework realizes that model with its own reactive primitive.

The kernel owns meaning. A framework package owns only the cheapest correct way to make that meaning observable. That is why these are realization layers rather than wrappers, and why none of them forces the others through a shared lowest common denominator.

About those memory figures

They are released entity-realization residue — what a collection still retains per entity after realized rows are dropped — measured on one harness, process-isolated, and reproduced at two entity counts. They are not a general speed or footprint claim.

CPU is unresolved. Differences could not be measured on the available hardware under the preregistered methodology, so no claim is made in either direction. Solid's realization cost has not been characterized at all; it shipped on correctness and semantic conformance.

These numbers should not be compared against SignalTree v14. v14's keys are identity, so a held node follows a fresh occupant of the same key — materially weaker guarantees, and a comparison without that sentence misrepresents both.
