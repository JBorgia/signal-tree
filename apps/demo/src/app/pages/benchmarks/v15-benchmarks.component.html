Methodology and sources

Each implementation keeps one populated store across warmup and measured rounds. Construction is outside the timer. Arm order rotates and reverses; every result must pass its correctness check. Stores are disposed after each workload.

Browser timing near the 0.1 ms floor is diagnostic. Overlapping observed ranges do not establish a winner; these are not statistical significance tests. Keep the tab foreground and rerun on your target device.

History results exclude asymmetric inspection materialization. No workload is pooled into an aggregate score or extrapolated to an assumed application frequency.

SignalTree runs the code bundled with this demo; version labels come from package manifests.
