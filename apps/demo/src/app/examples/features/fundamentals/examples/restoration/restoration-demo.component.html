Restoration retains only explicitly designated authored work. Ordinary writes remain authored but do not enter restoration history. Wrap a synchronous product operation in undoable() when undo and redo are part of that operation's contract.

The v15 model

  1. Authority first: application decisions are authored; values decided elsewhere are applied through external().
  2. Designation is separate: undoable() marks the authored causal turn containing its writes. It does not create a turn boundary.
  3. Settlement retains the turn: accepted designated work enters bounded restoration history after the causal turn settles.
  4. Undo and redo preserve authority: restoration may refuse rather than discard later external truth.
  5. Tooling can navigate: jumpTo() moves to another retained boundary without making snapshots causal authority.
