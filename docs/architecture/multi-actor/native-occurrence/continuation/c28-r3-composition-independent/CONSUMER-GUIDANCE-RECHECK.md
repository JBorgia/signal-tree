# Current draft recheck

Read-only contract review. No runtime work, root edits, or execution claims. The current draft retains the material ambiguities identified in CONSUMER-GUIDANCE-REVIEW.md. Its core current-obligation no-write rule is accurate; it is not yet complete promotion-ready guidance.

1. Draft “earlier receipt continues to represent its eventual send” must remain conditional on the approved unchanged A/B scenario. Generally an earlier operation may be superseded/disposed or its endpoint may fail. Say it still represents existing publication work, not a guaranteed eventual send. CURRENT-SPEC receipt section makes started endpoint invocation, not acknowledgment, and terminal receipts sticky.
2. “Use Link’s delivery lifecycle to wait for delivery” can mislead despite the following no-ack disclaimer. Name settled() as waiting for delivery work to settle, with failures through onTreeError; it resolves even when set rejects (composition link.ts120–123). Neither started nor settled establishes successful persistence. Do not imply automatic retry guarantees beyond the qualified endpoint contract.
3. “Every contributing transaction handle when it consumes pending work” is incomplete. CURRENT-SPEC requires every possible contributor, including saved/control inputs. C28-AUTHORITY explicitly preserves normal validation of freshly supplied confirmed handles and nested inputs, and inputs captured pending do not become obsolete just because they confirm during this operation. Caller responsibility is not limited to handles still pending at the end. Unknown grants no early declaration permission. A declaration cannot bless an old saved snapshot.
4. Explicitly reject async/thenable callback returns. A later continuation needs a new declaration, but that does not authorize returning a Promise now. Declaration failure is not rollback: writes may remain, and a throwing call returns no receipt. These are approved public boundaries, not internal implementation details.
5. Identify the unchanged existing owned-source boundary and link the source restrictions. No foreign/computed/custom source admission follows from this guide. Keep this brief; no proof machinery is necessary in consumer prose.

Promotion checks are a useful minimum, not a sufficient checklist or activation authority. Their explicit reference to remaining full qualification, size, large-workload and lifecycle gates is correct. Add/reference the CURRENT-SPEC shipping qualification checklist rather than duplicating the entire controller: all included source kinds and five facade public/packed consumers; fresh-confirmed and pending-during-call controls; saved/control inputs; nested/thenable errors with installed-write behavior; immutable/sticky receipts and disposal/retention; failed transport with settled/error handling; exact exports/member restrictions. Tests must assert useful delivery and errors, not just receipt labels. Source-only success is insufficient, as the entity artifact regression demonstrates.

The opening promotion instruction must also require owner activation/release disposition: integration and qualification alone do not grant it. C28-AUTHORITY and existing-source AUTHORITY explicitly authorize implementation/qualification only. No new policy decision is required to fix these ambiguities; all derive from already approved contracts. The draft can remain a working summary, but these qualifications should precede consumer promotion.
