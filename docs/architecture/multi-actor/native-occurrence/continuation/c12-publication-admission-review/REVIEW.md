## 1. Facts that justify recipient-independent surviving publication

The classification's raw two representative C11 traces show the distinction concretely: secondary-recipient preparation has all four uncertainty causes false; source-native proof has empty destination/input/validation/possible sets, unknown=false and invalid=false. The secondary projection instead contains unknown=true and possible=[pending], even with projectionPending=false. These detailed traces are C11 evidence, not mislabeled C12 instrumentation. The relevant C12 mechanism remains visible at:

- `experimental-publication/lifecycle.ts:253–302`: no matching channel frame makes `op.incomplete` true; finalization adds that recipient's unrelated pending window; `recipientProof` retains it on recomposition.
- `experimental-publication/private.ts:243–286`: the recipient operation proof is combined into installed support, so the artificial window contaminates the projection before final send checking. Merely ignoring a flag at send time is insufficient.
- `private.ts:446–466`: clean/early and conservative surviving admission are separate routes. `started` reports declared unless the actual surviving grant was used. Simply clearing unknown would risk misclassifying another recipient's send as declared.

Sufficient facts for the **bounded clean case**, all required together:

1. Exact source-installed ordinary revision and coherent selected payload, including every exposed field, membership/exclusion, key and ordering contributor. One clean newly written scalar does not certify a whole root with other held contributors.
2. Its native requirement is finalized **and activated**, after the enclosing synchronous validation horizon closes. A pre-unwind clean-looking proof is provisional; enrollment inside an updater must retain the live requirement until close.
3. Authentic destination/input/shared-validation obligations remain represented; native uncertainty, unavailable input, invalid proof, rejected roles and historical possible-owner windows are not dropped. The simple six-case witness has none of these obligations. More general terminal settlement remains governed by existing rules, not a new “all zero now” shortcut.
4. Recipient selection is complete and current: no genuine unobserved-origin/invalid-attribution/uncaptured-input/domain uncertainty, pending installed units or unresolved occurrences. Missing declaration ownership for this channel is distinct from missing source evidence.
5. Existing ordinary durable admission, current-revision preflight, disposal, endpoint reentry, supersession and serialized delivery checks still permit the send. No transaction outcome or recovery responsibility changes.

The declaring Link's callback is not a transferable grant. The installed source requirement records the actual execution/input/validation facts for its revision; another Link can select those facts and pass its **ordinary surviving** gate. It acquires no declaration receipt, matching frame, reusable trust bit or permission for future writes. In the six fixtures, the first receipt may start as declared while the second send must be recorded as surviving, with q still pending and unchanged.

### Bounded mechanism

Separate **absence of channel-local declaration permission** from **absence of source authorship evidence** at `prepareRevision`/`recipientProof`. Preserve the reason for uncertainty rather than reconstructing it from an already-combined unknown bit. Where exact installed source evidence is complete, do not manufacture a new historical window solely because this recipient has no matching declaration frame. Preserve every genuine uncertainty reason and inherited native window.

Compose selected recipient support from those source requirements and genuine recipient-domain facts, not from a receipt-only incompleteness proof that feeds its own artificial window back into support. Keep matching-channel declaration receipts separate. At `admit`/`allowed`/`started`, route a nondeclaring recipient through the existing ordinary gate and label its actual start surviving; do not reuse the declaring recipient's early permission. Revalidate the same current revision at the endpoint boundary. These are seams to implement and falsify, not a tested patch or permission to remove `incomplete` globally.

Legacy/uncovered scalar notifications must preserve any enrolled native support. The separately demonstrated C12 fallback replaces invalid pending support with a generic unknown proof lacking those obligations and sends surviving. A progress correction that also tolerates that safety loss is not qualified. No new global owner registry, borrowed current pending count, or notification-time reconstruction is justified.

## 2. Is equal explicit whole-write admission already specified?

**Partly, but not enough to close this exact failure as a mechanical oracle change or a fully specified implementation fix.** Three different cases must remain distinct:

| Case | Existing evidence/authority |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary7, inspected physical99, explicit ordinary99 | Owner-approved adoption already specified in C12 `RELEASE-CURRENT.md`, “Equal inspected-value adoption — owner decision”: rejection restores both physical and publication authority7. No new owner decision is needed for that existing rule. |
| Equal explicit field replacing a foreign possible-window hold, with no native destination owner | C12 `native-admission-handoff.spec.ts` positively passes. It also separately requires equal writes not to erase an actual destination owner's obligation. |
| Physically AND ordinarily equal whole row, historical field support still held, branch re-exposed | Original progress expectation exists, but C12 emits whole footprint plus fields=[] and retains the old field requirement. The exact certificate-only whole-field admission rule is not established by the two rows above. |

The retained restore has no ordinary/physical inspection divergence to adopt: x is already 7 in both. C11 raw trace records the identical x requirement before/after restore, unknown=true and possible=[q], with no native destination owner. Its packet contains `authoredFootprint:{kind:'whole'}` and ordinary fields=[]. Exact C12 repeats the outgoing hold while installing the requested live payload. This is not a payload/currentness failure or missed readiness wake-up.

Source boundary: `entity-signal.ts:2515–2527` represents authored scope separately from changed publication fields; the frame route derives the latter from differences. `experimental-publication/entity.ts:167–195` replaces endpoints only for those fields. Native responsibility selection (`enhancers/transactions/transactions.ts:2472–2483,2626–2631`) can preserve an owned prior endpoint or choose an admitted proposal. Expanding the publication field list directly from `whole` without that choice would bypass the admission boundary.

`native-authored-footprint.spec.ts` explicitly states “Scope is not yet ownership/admission.” The declaration draft's “A later authored revision gets new evidence, even at equal value” describes an **authored revision**, not proof that every equal invocation is admitted. The document explicitly leaves edge policies unapproved. The older equal-setter no-op recommendation is likewise not current authority for inspection adoption: the later owner approval supersedes it. Neither historical document settles this narrower whole-row certificate-only case.

### Minimal admission proposal and genuine decision boundary

If explicit whole replacement is intended to reauthor equal unowned fields, its bounded implementation must offer the explicit whole footprint to **native field admission**, preserving lifetime, own-property presence/absence and operation order. It should select a new source-issued endpoint only where admission accepts new authorship; preserve an existing endpoint where destination ownership or inverse/net-zero rules require it. Partial/derive/realization paths must not acquire whole-row scope. Recipients then consume the selected endpoints normally; no Link-only relabeling of the retained payload.

The unresolved semantic choice is precisely: **does an admitted explicit whole replacement reauthor each equal unowned field's publication support, as an explicit field write can, even when no ordinary value changes?** The narrow affirmative rule is consistent with the failing progress oracle and existing explicit-field control; it is not established merely by a whole footprint. If Main has a later approved admission contract covering it, apply that existing authority rather than requesting it again. In the supplied C12 records I did not find that exact rule. Selecting it absent such a record is the genuine owner/contract decision; arbitrary declaration API design or broad causal-model changes are not needed to answer it.

Do not reopen the already-approved inspection-adoption decision, and do not conflate this with transferring transaction ownership. A declaration never clears an actual pending destination owner's claim; the passing equal/inverse/net-zero controls remain mandatory. Characterizing the current conservative hold would not satisfy the original useful-progress expectation.

## 3. Discriminators for a concrete successor

### Recipient-independent progress and safety

- Keep all six original enrollment cases and their basis assertions unchanged. Require one secondary send before q settles, exact source payload, no q.inspect change; first Link declared, second surviving. Cover enrollment before/during pending and inside updater, plus existing disposal variants.
- Remove the declaration / supply unknown input: a source's real authorship window must still hold. A clean scalar alongside an untouched foreign-held scalar or held membership/order contributor must not release the whole projection.
- Enroll before horizon close, then throw or introduce a real nested contradiction. Reuse the separate **uninstrumented C12 parent-invalidation** falsifier and exercise queued uncovered scalar notification delivery. No early surviving escape; later settlement must still make useful progress.
- Change enrollment or source revision during preparation/completion, dispose at preflight, or start a newer queued write: no stale payload/certificate pairing or resurrected recipient. Existing readiness activation must be positively observed, not mocked by finalization alone.

### Equal whole admission

- Paired equal `updateOne({x:7})`, `replaceOne(whole7)`, `setAll([whole7])`, and root omission/re-exposure. Record admitted field endpoint identity, D/I/V/W proof roles, native effects and actual outgoing payload; do not assert only `fields.length` or footprint.
- Contrast foreign possible-window-only support with an actual destination-owned x=7. The former progresses if the narrow rule is selected; the latter remains held and rolls back to the existing baseline. Keep current equal/inverse/net-zero handoff controls unchanged.
- Whole replacement with absent versus present-undefined fields; same-key new lifetime; partial/derive preserving an untouched held sibling; pure reorder/realization acquiring no new value authorship. Do not merge these semantics just because final payloads compare equal.
- Refused admission or later preparer throw leaves the outer attempt uninstalled; independent reentrant work remains. Confirmation, rollback, no-op notification identity and recipient enrollment order must agree with the selected native endpoints. No extra value notification solely to announce certificate work.
