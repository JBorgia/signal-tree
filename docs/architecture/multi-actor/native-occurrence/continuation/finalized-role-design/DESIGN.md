## Why the inventory stops here

There is a precise safe entry point for scalar enrollment (installed native completion), and concrete recipient cancellation sites. There is **not yet an existing cancellation owner for a new shared source-normalization listener** covering nativeValues, physical views, inverse endpoints, saved snapshots and superseded aliases.

onNativeRequirementReady (lifecycle.ts:128–141) returns an idempotent release and releases before callback, but its check closure strongly captures the requirement. Attaching it during combine/preparation allows an old unfinished child to retain a never-installed parent after a later preparer throws. Weak maps around that registration do not remove the strong edge. Waiting for installation prevents that particular failed-preparation leak, but does not by itself decide cancellation when one source slot or recipient is replaced while other aliases survive.

Cancelling on Link.dispose is too early for surviving source support; never cancelling until owner settlement is wrong because owner outcome is not requirement readiness. Using source unregister/owner abandonment as cancellation would conflate publication-source storage with requirement lifetime. A horizon-owned registration that always terminates at validation/activation closure could be an alternative, but requires a concrete guarantee for installed completion, finish-before-activation and error paths, plus a specified temporary retention bound. That ownership rule is not supplied by the current participant interface or these reviews. This document does not select it, add weak-reference machinery, or introduce a reference-counted lifetime policy to fill the gap.

Accordingly the bounded implementation verdict remains **not closed**, not “impossible.” Do not treat immediate all-ready normalization or recipient-only normalization as solving the idle no-recipient installed-source case. Exact missing work is a source/alias-safe lifetime for the post-install readiness registration, and explicit disposition of entity completion.support. Stop at those seams rather than expand owner retirement or add a representation-wide ownership system.
