## Fresh admission versus settlement sweep

In the executed sequence, each new qi is admitted while p persists, then qi confirms before the next admission. On admission i+1, historical confirmed qi can be omitted while current pending p and qi+1 remain. That bounds this CURRENT-certificate history by the obligations retained at the most recent admission, without a settlement observer, global scan or retroactive alias mutation. After the final qi confirms, a small terminal tail may remain until another admission/source replacement; that is compatible with this stated boundary.

This is NOT a bound proportional only to currently pending owners for all workloads. A single admission can legitimately include many pending contributors, which can later confirm while the source is idle. Fresh-admission-only cleanup leaves that finite certificate intact. A stronger idle-reclamation requirement would need separate authority/semantics. Do not infer necessity of a sweep from the sequential trace, or infer general L15 from eliminating that trace.
