    test(v16): 8d(c) review follow-up: count searches and key listings too
    
    The second review pass found the iteration counter blind to whole-array
    searches (`indexOf`, `includes`, `find` and the rest) and to `Object.keys`,
    `values` and `entries`, so such an O(size) regression would pass. They are
    counted now at their full length. Mutations adding an `indexOf` over the key
    snapshot to `updateOne` and an `Object.keys` over the slots to a compiled
    write are killed.
    
    Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>
