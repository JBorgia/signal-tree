## Three different uncertainties

1. **Native input requirements.** The runtime itself supplies an updater argument. Its complete observation domain includes ordinary values and ordinary presence/absence/topology facts actually supplied/observable in that argument. Known authentic owners and unavailable/invalid prerequisites are factual lower bounds. They are unaffected by recipient enrollment/disposal and cannot be erased by an independent declaration. Masked descendant values need not contribute merely because their retained handles exist; their membership absence can still be observed by hasOwn, keys or branch topology.
2. **Recipient coverage uncertainty.** The source may have a complete native-input certificate while a particular Link lacks its own historical/coverage annotation. This prevents declared early permission for that recipient, but does not create a foreign dependency or contaminate another recipient. A fresh coherent ordinary projection can use its supported ordinary gate when native/foreign constraints permit it.
3. **Unobserved origin.** An ordinary setter receives a value, but no complete declaration or native-input scope explains where it came from. This includes both literal7 and `foreign.x()` returning7. They have identical target-side metadata. Unknown origin cannot be converted to independence by equality, source registry absence, another Link's permission, or an empty target-tree gate.

The sixth wider-root fixture belongs to2 when the new native input mechanism genuinely certifies its supplied local root: foreign p is unrelated to that argument. The unchanged direct getter copy belongs to3, and actually contains speculative data. These are not interchangeable causes of one unknown bit.

## Genuine incompatibility to resolve explicitly

These three statements cannot all hold for the indistinguishable unscoped operations:

- every ordinary unknown-origin write immediately uses only the target-tree gate;
- targetField(7) progresses while unrelated foreign p is open;
- targetField(foreign.x()) stays held while that same foreign p is open.

Without another observable execution boundary, no implementation can distinguish the last two. Native updater certificates and complete declarations distinguish some supported operations, but do not cover arbitrary top-level getter expressions. Complete declarations assign hidden-input omissions to the application; the default witness makes no such declaration.

The existing production consequence gate is deliberately tree-local; that fact never proved transitive cross-tree safety. The preserved experimental safety control and the owner's refusal to treat unsupported copying as permission add a stricter obligation for unobserved origins. A finite coherent contract is possible by **qualifying the ordinary fallback by evidence class**, but that qualification must be explicit. If “tree-local fallback for every unknown-origin setter” remains absolute, the direct-copy no-send requirement needs an owner scope change or additional read/execution observation. Do not solve this by silently reclassifying the unchanged safety assertion.

For the third row, choosing all currently pending **instrumented** owner cells is a conservative possible-owner policy that satisfies the direct-copy pre-confirm hold and permits its all-confirm wake. It also holds some genuinely unrelated unscoped writes. That is a deliberate liveness tradeoff; useful immediate progress still comes from the qualified native/declaration paths. It is not the old global uncertainty rule applied to recipient-only coverage, and must not pollute common native requirements or validation frames.

The owner must approve/confirm this exact unobserved-origin qualification if it is intended to replace the previously broad tree-local fallback interpretation. ### Confirmation and wake

The complete unchanged direct-copy assertion requires a real wake: confirming the relevant window must invalidate eligibility independently of value notifications, enqueue the held exact source revision, and evaluate a fresh coherent ordinary projection after terminal effects/validation close. It must not merely remove p from a set or wait for another write. The prerequisite is a source/reducer path capable of supplying O truth; the entity field remains an unmigrated fallback producer in the frozen source. Rereading an inspection-visible physical field is not a valid substitute. Eligibility and delivery remain separate, with the already-proved serialized corrective-send state machine.

A late owner is not retroactively attached to an immutable earlier installed value/window. It can affect a fresh source revision, required structural fact, or existing local conservative gate. A wake never reuses an old grant for a changed payload. If the source revision changes during preparation/delivery, reprepare or supersede; do not transfer permission by equality.

### Rejection

Rejection invalidates the old speculative permission and triggers fresh O evaluation. It does not confirm the window or authorize sending the captured7 merely because pending count reached zero. If a required surviving fact has rejected/unavailable origin and no new evidence replaces it, hold with an explicit reason or require fresh supported authorship; do not silently cleanse it. This conservative disposition may delay an actually independent unscoped write that happened in the same possible-owner window. Whether a completely drained rejected possible-owner window may instead establish a fresh ordinary baseline is **not resolved by the all-confirm witness** and must be specified before implementation.

The approved saved-value rule is separate and concrete: read pending7, reject its source, then ordinarily write retained7; that later write is surviving new application-authored truth. Do not retain the old transaction as its rollback owner or automatically inherit rejected causality forever. This does not retroactively authorize a pre-rejection captured ticket. A fresh complete independent setter/declaration after rejection is the explicit positive control; no value event may be forced merely to refresh publication responsibility.

### Destruction, unavailable sources and saved inputs

Destroying a source or losing its registry is not confirmation. Authentic outcome cells already referenced by support/windows must remain readable for the live obligation; source→tree references and callbacks must detach according to existing cleanup rules. Unknown unavailable foreign support stays conservative. Explicit connection disposal separately resolves its own held work. No auto-drain guarantee follows from tree destruction alone.

A current possible-owner window is not a universal proof of arbitrary retained-value provenance, another runtime instance, uninstrumented writes or async work. Those limits require a documented supported boundary; a complete declaration remains the application's means of accepting hidden inputs. The approved post-rejection surviving-write semantics deliberately do not demand recovering arbitrary historical causality. Never describe this policy as general taint tracking.

## Targeted rejection adjudication — correction to the initial qualification

The all-terminal condition in the root's follow-up **is already permitted**; no additional owner choice is needed merely to allow fresh surviving-basis fallback after a possible owner rejects. My earlier statement that this particular disposition remained unresolved was too restrictive. `FALLBACK-PREMISES-FIRST.md` preserves that original analysis rather than silently rewriting it.

Controlling approved A0:78 says exactly:

> Rejection | Invalidate the old all-confirm proof. Selected inverse units update current facts first. A fresh conservative evaluation of current surviving ordinary projection may replace the old ticket when actual gates permit; rejection never confirms the captured speculative payload.

The earlier executable declaration contract is more explicit (`link-declaration-examples/SPEC.md:19–23`):

> At conservative drain obtain a NEW receipt from coherent current eligible projection, validate latest version before send. Do not adopt inspection state. Same surviving numeric value may send under fresh ordinary-truth evaluation; equality is not proof.

Its fixed examples expressly include “rejection leaves ordinary copied target surviving” and “rejection with another owner still pending” (SPEC:30–32). The corresponding README:9–14 records restoration sends0 instead of old7, while an ordinary surviving7 may send7 under a different evaluation basis. This separates new projection evaluation from confirmation of old origin. A new post-rejection ordinary write is an additional positive case, **not a prerequisite for every surviving fallback**.

Therefore, when every captured possible owner has an authentic terminal outcome, all relevant validation has finished, no known unresolved/unavailable source requirement remains, and the source supplies a coherent current ordinary projection, the finite rule can be:

1. Invalidate/retire the old ticket and its early/all-confirm permission.
2. Finish selected inverse/source projection installation.
3. Reevaluate current surviving O under the actual conservative gates and current revision.
4. If those gates pass, admit a **new surviving-basis ticket**, even if its numeric value equals the rejected ticket's payload. Retain separate identity/basis and validate again before I/O.

This is permitted ordinary fallback, not laundering a rejected proof into confirmation. Requiring a fresh complete authored replacement or disposal after **every** rejection would narrow the already approved surviving-copy contract. The implementation still has to demonstrate the O source, fresh ticket, permission basis and useful wake; dropping the rejected owner from an old ticket is insufficient.

Unavailable and destroyed are different. A source disappearing from a registry is not a terminal outcome; a still-unresolved captured owner is not “all terminal” merely because enumeration no longer finds it. A0:137 expressly retains known foreign unavailable conservatism and says source destruction alone is not permission to send. Authentic already-recorded confirmation or rollback remains terminal after registry cleanup; unavailable/unverifiable outcomes do not. Explicit connection disposal has its separate cancellation/waiter contract.

The remaining policy choice in the main report is **which unobserved-origin writes receive a possible-owner window at admission, and its supported completeness boundary**. The rejection behavior after the stated genuine all-terminal/coherent-O condition is not another open policy choice. Unknown historical or unmapped source facts still cannot be relabeled known; those facts determine whether the stated prerequisites actually hold.
