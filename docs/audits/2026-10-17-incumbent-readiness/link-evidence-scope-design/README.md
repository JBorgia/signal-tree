## Mechanism and verified interpretation

[Source lifecycle inventory](SOURCE-LIFECYCLE-MAP.md) traces creation, combination,
replacement and retirement. Existing recipient-keyed input snapshots lose scope
at private admission: attributed native owners and recipient-associated proof
owners are unioned, and recipient unknown coverage is broadcast into every active
validation frame. Passive enrollment can consequently change another recipient's
permission. The root agent verified these joins directly in frozen
private.ts:155–164 and lifecycle.ts:86–102.

The proposed replacement preserves provenance from preparation through completion
and retirement. Shared native requirements require source/footprint evidence;
actual nested declarations continue accumulating under A0. Recipient unknown
coverage remains local and cannot mint or revoke another recipient's grant.
Admission, native completion, validation closure, projection and delivery basis
remain distinct. Exact scalar member routing uses source identity and relation,
not an aggregate-only descendant rule. This explains the original four missing
scalar membership publications without changing their permission policy.

The review challenged nested scopes, native proof versus owner authentication,
replacement, fallback, teardown and omitted producers. Its remaining obligations
are incorporated in the design. In particular, public updateAndReport(updater)
supplies a native root argument without the callable updater's input scope. Both
reviewer and root verified that source fact; no executable failure or repair is
claimed. Authentic owner identity alone does not prove a dependency on that owner.
No new public-semantic decision was found necessary for this design.
