1. **Additional read/execution observation.** Define a supported synchronous
   execution boundary that records actual native reads and carries them to
   writes. This is new work beyond the approved updater-input/source-record
   mapping. It needs rules for ordinary top-level calls, saved values, reentry,

   hidden-input responsibility, while unscoped ordinary copies follow surviving
   application-write/tree-local semantics. This would change the preserved
   cross-tree control's safety claim and requires owner authority; it is not
   authorized by the unrelated-owner fallback correction.

Recommendation: reject this successor as qualified integration, retain both exact
failures, and obtain the narrow scope decision before additional runtime work.
Do not disguise the policy conflict with an entity-only guard, value comparison,
connection-specific exception, or new successful test count. The source-owned
requirement representation remains useful evidence for observable updater inputs;
it cannot manufacture the missing direct-read causal edge.
