## Authorities and units

Five units must not be conflated:

1. **Source installed unit.** The maximal existing callback-free installation interval whose preconditions can be validated together. It has an immutable identity and sequence, exact authored footprint, separate physical P and ordinary O changes, participation/origin, selected inverse identity when applicable, and an optional prepared publication-support edit. Installation of state, incumbent settlement responsibility and support edit occurs within that interval, before any capture sink/publication can run.
2. **Logical native operation.** A method can install several units. Earlier siblings may survive a later preparation failure. Native scalar capture currently runs after value installation and before path re-addition. Preserve those two intervals; do not move membership ahead of capture. A reentrant callback creates new units, even when the same transaction owns them.
3. **Declaration horizon.** Synchronous validation, nested declarations, return-shape getters and diagnostic reentry must all finish before any covered unit obtains early permission. The horizon gates permission; it does not coalesce units, assign rollback ownership or make callbacks atomic.
4. **Relationship projection revision.** An immutable completed revision of one Link's eligible projection, built from ordered installed units. It may contain facts from multiple operations and dependencies. The relationship creation baseline stays relationship-relative; it is not a new native ordinary store.
5. **Delivery attempt.** A started endpoint request uses one immutable projection revision. Later writes cannot mutate it. Existing serialized delivery and corrective-send rules continue.

Native state remains current-state authority; incumbent transaction/history selection remains inverse authority. The proposed support index is an additional **publication eligibility authority**, and is acknowledged as such. It stores fact identity and evidence, not values from which native state could be reconstructed or settled. Values are retained only in the existing eligible projection, existing history/capture, prepared deltas and queued/in-flight payloads for their existing lifetimes.

## Fact address and support representation

A relationship has a stable baseline plus sparse overrides indexed by native identity:

- Scalar/field value: owned position and entity lifetime where applicable, never a string path alone.
- Ordinary member presence: parent identity plus literal member key, presence generation and existing child identity. Absence is a fact, not an undefined value.
- Entity existence and business-key occupancy: lifetime and mapping facts separately. Payload `id` is not a storage-key authority.
- Ordering: exact authored order footprint from the existing ordinary-order owner. A full reorder covers its explicit domain; a subset reorder covers its specified slots/lifetimes while omitted eligible subjects retain their slots. Neighbor observations used for preparation do not confer authorship.

Each live fact points to an immutable proof: installed-unit identity, connection coverage, declared authentic owner cells, known contributing owner cells, unknown marker, and enclosing validation-horizon identities. Owner outcomes are irreversible. Unknown is not an empty owner set. No dependency array remains mutable through caller objects.

Proofs share immutable internal records by identity, not by hashing application payloads and not by copying one cumulative map per operation. The relationship tracks the blocked/validating counts and dependency-to-fact reverse links needed to update eligibility incrementally. A point edit updates its facts and actual dependents, not every registered tree, collection or operation. A whole output or full reorder may process its specified domain. No CPU or byte advantage is claimed until measured.

Baseline creation adopts the current read under the existing relationship-creation contract, including inspection predating creation. It neither sends initially nor grants independence. If creation occurs when the existing conservative gate permits that coherent relationship baseline and no known foreign support prevents it, its already-established facts receive baseline-safe support. This is evidence for that baseline, not an independence declaration or initial send. Open unrelated transactions later do not retroactively contaminate those facts. The required positive control creates the connection in that state, then opens x and independently replaces existing y. If creation occurs while relevant pending work prevents establishing the baseline and trustworthy pre-creation support is unavailable, baseline evidence is unknown. No early-progress claim is made for that unavailable-evidence case. A later declaration covers newly authored facts only. It cannot bless untouched baseline structure. The default conservative release can establish a new relationship baseline only from a fresh coherent surviving projection after its actual gates pass.

## Evidence algebra and replacement rules

For a newly installed authored fact F, preparation computes its proof from (a) declaration coverage at that unit, (b) known native inputs required by the operation, and (c) unresolved structural facts required to expose F. The latter remain separately indexed rather than copied into every descendant. Preparation reads are not automatically authored facts or dependencies. A native updater receiving a current source value has a known input; that is distinct from an arbitrary hidden application read which the runtime cannot observe. Source requirements, such as target lifetime existence, are not incidental reads and cannot be erased by a label.

A valid independent declaration contributes no *declared* owners; it cannot clear known support. A dependent declaration contributes its copied identities. No relevant declaration or unknown/invalid enclosing context contributes unknown. A transaction's actual authorship remains a known lower bound; a publication declaration never converts a pending transaction's write into committed work.

Permission for a payload requires every still-contributing fact and relevant validation horizon to permit it. A scalar source still requires its exposed member/lifetime to exist. An aggregate uses all contributing fields and structural facts. Replacing one value proof does not replace the aggregate's proof.

A later independent overwrite may remove a value dependency when it actually replaces that value. It cannot remove a required lifetime or membership dependency. A fresh scope after a dependent operation can establish a complete replacement; an inner declaration cannot erase inherited unknown/invalid obligations. Reentrant/newer writes invalidate stale prepared revisions; they are not overwritten by the older unit's deferred publication.

### Nested dependency inheritance

Preserve the approved accumulating declaration-scope rule. Declared and known child obligations merge into the enclosing declaration's known set; an outer independent → inner dependent(p) y-write → outer literal y-write accumulates known p. The outer immutable complete-empty assertion is therefore contradictory: it throws the approved after-callback declaration error, retains installed writes conservatively and cannot publish early. The rejected native candidate's valid-held result is not the acceptance oracle. An inherited unknown or invalid scope cannot be cleared by a later setter. Keep each frame's copied declared set separate from its accumulated known set. Validate the latter against the former at horizon close; a nested declaration does not silently amend an outer complete assertion. Freeze the resulting evidence, then associate it with that frame's still-contributing admitted facts. If the callback also threw, preserve that original value and report the declaration error secondarily under the existing contract. Shared validation failure prevents early permission across relevant connections. Do not mutate owner identities or turn rejection into confirmation.

Per-fact support still matters across separate operations/scopes: x retains its prior dependent proof while a later independent operation changes y; a fresh complete overwrite outside the closed horizon replaces exactly its authored facts. This is the required mixed-payload discriminator. No new narrower per-child propagation policy is selected. Frame/horizon accumulation is validation-time evidence with bounded lifetime, not a permanent transaction owner or canonical value store.

## Installation and composition protocol

1. Identify the exact source-unit footprint, source revision and participating relationship bindings through affected-source indexes. An explicit setter may require a support-only unit even when the state equality path skips value installation; compare effective proof inputs before that early return. Do not force this work onto an unobserved bare tree. Use existing native identity and ordinary-order owners.
2. Run equality, updater/shape reflection and user-controlled validation under the existing timing. Prepare incumbent responsibility plus private support edits/queue nodes and native descriptor/lifetime/index edits. Validate revisions after anything that can reenter. No new reachable resource is installed on failed preparation.
3. In the existing callback-free interval, install the native unit, settlement responsibility and support edit, and register pending projection work. This registration is necessary even when no public value event will follow. Supported failure sites are equality/updaters, snapshot/reflection, responsibility preparation, recipient/delta preparation and validation. They run before this unit installs. Commit only prepared internal records and primitive/native identity indexes; no user callback, clone or reducer that can invoke user code may occur in that interval. A test throwing at the former rich-capture boundary remains required after installation: the minimal installed responsibility and support must already survive it. Arbitrary process-OOM inside primitive storage mutation is outside this claim, not a reason to exclude ordinary throwing preparation or callback cases. Prepared pure projection deltas require no post-install user reflection; observer/transport errors cannot delete unreduced work or change ownership terminality.
4. Preserve existing capture timing. If capture separates value installation from membership, it sees the same native timing as before; the first unit already has support. Reprepare the later membership unit if reentry changed its preconditions. A later failure cannot retroactively make the earlier installed unit unowned. Existing abort/recovery handles earlier partial success.
5. Complete each source unit once on the private path port; apply its exact O delta to each relevant relationship in installed sequence. Public path notifications keep their existing membership/value chronology and coalescing. They no longer create or complete eligibility evidence.
6. Close the existing logical native operation only after its actual constituent installations finish, or with a partial-installed disposition on failure. Reentry has its own operation identity. Compose ordinary membership and order across the complete selected native operation before capture/history/publication consumers accept its final domain; an intermediate snapshot cannot stand for the whole operation.
7. Release scheduling only at the existing causal/consequence boundary and after relevant validation horizons close. Native group timing does not promise fresh application-owned computed values inside groups.

Structural realization must prepare its whole selected inverse domain before any installation, as already required for atomic settlement. Logical ordinary methods that presently allow partial success do not acquire settlement's all-or-none rule. An explicit operation membership list describes only the actual composed units, not an entire ambient transaction. No commit is inferred from public event count or a matching final value.

## Completion and delivery state machine

Pending work is created by an installed relevant unit, not a public value event. It has installed sequence, projection target revision, outstanding reduction count and validation/gate obligations. Multiple units coalesce only after applying their exact deltas. A net-zero public value still completes work and responsibility revision; it need not send a duplicate payload if acknowledged equality and the ordinary Link contract suppress it.

- **Registered/incomplete:** settled waiters can see work; no endpoint invocation. Installed units must be reduced or explicitly marked irrelevant/disposed. Failure cannot strand an untracked gap.
- **Complete/held:** latest projection is coherent, but support/horizon/conservative gate blocks. Owner-to-fact and horizon-to-work indexes wake this entry even if no send has started and held-send count was previously zero.
- **Complete/eligible:** immutable packet captures exact projection revision and support revision. Revalidate both at the existing send boundary. Synchronous reentry superseding either discards that unstarted packet and schedules the latest, not a different value under old permission.
- **Started:** immutable request remains serialized. A later held value does not erase a later eligible corrective value. Request completion acknowledges only its own revision; it cannot mark newer work clean.
- **Superseded:** unstarted packet releases its payload/claims after replacing it with registered current work. Previously started receipts stay started.
- **Disposed:** cancel unstarted connection work and release its records/waiters under existing disposal semantics. Started request may finish but cannot revive the connection or enqueue new work.

Tree destruction does **not** implicitly dispose Link. Preserve the exact distinction traced in DESTRUCTION-MAP.md:

- Explicit connection disposal cancels unstarted work and resolves local settled waiters before user cleanup. An already-started endpoint request is not canceled; it cannot restart a disposed connection.
- Destroying the source with a connection retained does not acknowledge or cancel its already-started request; existing settled waits until that request finishes. The existing queued-source-destroy witness allows zero or one pre-destroy value; A0 does not strengthen that into deterministic cancellation.
- Unavailable owner sources never imply confirmation. Registry availability and irreversible settlement outcome are distinct facts. Removal from the source registry makes unresolved/unverifiable evidence unavailable to early authorization; it does not fabricate rolled-back/confirmed outcomes or downgrade an authentic retained confirmed outcome. Known terminal confirmation remains admissible even when its original source registry entry is gone. Known foreign unavailable support remains conservative; native source destruction alone is not permission to send it.
- The preserved held-destruction witness establishes only that explicit connection disposal releases its waiter. Automatic progress after source destruction is **not established**. The supported recovery paths for unavailable publication evidence are a newly authored replacement with complete valid evidence for all affected facts, where the source still permits that write, or explicit connection disposal. No restore/settlement authority is fabricated for a destroyed transaction.
- Live retained connections may keep compact blocked-work/evidence records; teardown qualification disposes those connections as well as destroying their source. It must prove that neither user-retained terminal receipts nor unavailable owner cells retain a tree or historical graph after that cleanup. Source-destroy-alone and explicit-dispose results are reported separately, not pooled into a cleanup pass.

A requested guarantee that source destruction alone cancels every pending relationship and releases every waiter would be a separate public lifetime change. It is neither assumed nor needed to disguise a failing control here.

Receipt states and errors keep the approved surface. A receipt describes the last relevant authored revision of its operation, as the approved surface requires. Internal support references may cover many contributing facts; they do not turn the receipt into a multi-revision delivery status. The receipt keeps only that revision identity and detached result; eligibility derives from all facts still needed by that revision. It is terminal at started/superseded/disposed/no-write. Later callbacks cannot mutate it. Its terminal snapshot contains no source tree, connection, owner handle, closure or historical payload graph. Live support, queued work and in-flight delivery own their own references independently; deleting a receipt never deletes necessary proof.
