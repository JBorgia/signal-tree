## Concrete composition, after selection

The native ordinary-order source owner supplies separate P/O continuity tokens and touched lifetime placement pairs at preparation. Each pair includes presence, key, before/after adjacency and touched head/tail. An explicit broad ordering operation also supplies its already-required before/after domain. These pairs are completion evidence, not additional authored inverse effects. A changed neighbor link does not make the neighbor owned by this writer.

A source installed unit commits the next applicable P/O token and evidence with its existing responsibility, before any capture listener can reenter. Its prepared terminal plan also admits the existing per-collection transaction order-interference witness at that same interval; later order-capture delivery acknowledges the installed unit idempotently. This replaces reliance on the later transactions.ts collection-order callback (2777–2807) for first witness admission, so an entity observer cannot author an intervening order before the outer witness exists. P-only inspection does not advance O. Ordinary-only adoption does. A field-only unit is topology-neutral; it must not overwrite a token-index edge with a self-edge. Entity and order captures from the same completed interval carry the same unit identity, so consumers do not double-apply its membership frame. Nested callback work gets a distinct identity and begins after the outer interval's completed native order.

The first ordinary endpoint retained at4447–4463 is extended together with the physical endpoint by the paired composer, not left as an intermediate domain. Native transaction selection stays in TransactionAuthority.getPendingRollbackPlan/buildPendingRollbackPlan and restoration's existing history selection. compileSelectedCompensation does not select every effect of a unit merely because its completion evidence was retained. The composer:

1. Receives only selected effects plus the source evidence required to understand their completed domains. Keeps the existing independent-order interference witness, including ABA.
2. Forms compatible contiguous selected runs separately in P and O. Missing O evidence is a preparation defect, never a fallback to physical neighbors. An unselected topology change breaks simple extension; value-only interference need not.
3. Folds touched pairs through proven continuity, preserving original before/final after and intermediate live inverse obligations. A transient anchor's net disappearance does not release its live claim.
4. Only where an existing selected order delta already requires a broad endpoint, extends its before domain through selected prefix changes and its after domain through selected suffix changes. Departing/arriving lifetimes and adjacency edits are resolved as a set against the proposed final topology before validation. No extra order authority is manufactured for a field/membership-only inverse.
5. Validates the completed order against completed membership. Applies existing successor/predecessor placement and occupied-destination rules; does not introduce historical bracket semantics or chronological replay. Missing optional frontier-bridge proof is not by itself a new refusal reason.
6. Supplies that same selected ordinary result and distinct physical realization footprint to incumbent inverse preparation, inspection and Link. Physical target values cannot broaden ordinary field authority. Installation remains atomic for settlement, preserving retry on preparation refusal.

## Failure and scheduling map

Prepare equality/updater/reflection/snapshot, responsibility, affected binding list and pure projection edit before each callback-free interval. Reentrant preparation invalidates the outer revision; installed nested work survives. Pure internal commit creates state, minimal responsibility, support and pending-reduction registration together. Rich capture may still throw afterward; it cannot erase those records. A later unit's preparation failure preserves the earlier installed prefix and existing abort/recovery behavior.

The optional path port receives installed-unit completion independent of public events. The source owns exactly-once unit completion; an event flush cannot complete unrelated units. Existing Link eligible reducers consume the immutable delta at the matching position/lifetime and preserve creation-baseline semantics. They must not call application getters or equality during the private commit; those operations are prepared/validated first or remain at their existing safe public stage.

Link's held/waiter mechanism is reused rather than adding a polling loop. Pending reduction owns a release signal before a public event or private completion can expose work. settled() checks that registration alongside existing retrieval, pending-order, held and chain work. Reduction completion schedules through the current consequence key even if held.size was zero. Only complete coherent packets can enter the serialized endpoint chain. Explicit disposal releases those new signals before user cleanup, just as it already releases held/waiting/order/retrieval signals.

No-endpoint or unrelated-source units do not register connection work. Connection creation after a unit cannot acquire its old declaration. Disposal during preparation invalidates that binding before commit; disposal after commit retires its pending work without touching other connections' authority. Source destruction and explicit connection disposal retain the distinction in DESTRUCTION-MAP.md.
