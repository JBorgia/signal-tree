# A1 aggregate declaration scope: proposed experiment, not approval

2026-10-26. A0 at readiness checkpoint 4ed3b609 requires a positive per-field aggregate replacement control (case 5). The previously approved API proposal explicitly limits declarations to scalar sources and forbids one scalar connection from certifying an aggregate. Independent interpretation review is AGGREGATE-SCOPE-INTERPRETATION.md. An object-valued scalar updater consumes its entire previous value; using it would not establish per-field support composition. This is a genuine scope gap in A0, not a reason to silently weaken case 5.

Proposed authorization: extend the *isolated experimental* `connection.declare(declaration, synchronousWrites)` to ordinary branch/root and entity-collection connections, with unchanged syntax, and explicitly define declaration completeness as **all inputs to newly authored facts**, rather than every already-contributing fact in the outgoing projection. That semantic clarification also applies to scalar/field sources with structural prerequisites. Known untouched siblings/ancestor support may make a valid operation held; they are not a false independence claim merely because they still contribute to the payload. Actual consumed native inputs and inherited validation evidence still require declaration and can contradict independence. This needs owner authority, not an internal implementation choice. No production type/export change, default activation, release/API acceptance or budget change. Existing scalar/field contracts stay unchanged.

- A declaration covers only actual installed ordinary facts authored by the callback within that connection's native source. Other connections do not inherit it. A no-write scope grants nothing.
- A field replacement updates only that field's publication support. Native value-input dependencies (including updater arguments) and enclosing validation obligations remain lower bounds on its declaration. Missing input declarations cause the established contradiction handling.
- Existing untouched field, ancestor presence, entity lifetime, occupancy and order facts retain their support. Their presence is not automatically an input read by a field replacement; they can hold the completed projection without making the field declaration false. A callback that actually consumes them must declare its inputs under the existing known/hidden-input rules.
- The outgoing projection and per-operation receipt remain conservative/held until every contributing fact and validation horizon permits it. No eligible receipt while that operation's relevant projection is held by another contributing fact. The receipt remains a view of the last relevant authored projection revision, not a transport acknowledgment or a mutable history of every intermediate write.
- Native full replacement may replace all the facts it actually authors. It does not automatically adopt unrelated inspection state, implicit ancestors, incidental order neighbors, or another lifetime. Rollback ownership and restoration are unchanged.
- Membership-only and support-only units schedule completion independently of value events. Installation/capture/inspection/publication consume the same selected ordinary facts and separate physical facts.

Prewritten discriminators (all through a single aggregate connection):

1. Baseline ordinary branch `{x:0,y:0}`; pending p owns x=7, with matching declared dependency. A later ordinary independent y=8 leaves aggregate held while y retains independent support. Its declaration is valid-but-held. The earlier x-operation receipt becomes superseded by the later relevant aggregate projection revision; the y receipt is held. Confirm p: useful `{x:7,y:8}` sends. Reject p: native rollback restores x=0; `{x:0,y:8}` can send only through valid support or the actual conservative fallback gate, never by releasing the old speculative ticket.
   Separate surviving-copy witness: p owns a different source; an ordinary x=7 copy declared dependent on p remains ordinary x=7 after p rejects. Rejection does not undo that copy or itself grant early permission. Existing conservative fallback must still respect another open owner q and known unresolved/unavailable foreign support. A newly authored complete independent replacement may progress while q remains pending if all untouched structural support is safe.
2. The same held branch then receives a complete independent replacement of both fields in a new scope. Useful replacement sends while p stays pending only if all unchanged structural support is already safe; this grants p no settlement confirmation. The prior y receipt becomes superseded; the new replacement receipt may become started. Terminal receipt states never revert even while required source support survives.
3. A pending ancestor/lifetime is not made independent by an independent field overwrite. Projection and receipt remain held. Native updater reading its pending value still creates a known-input contradiction if omitted from a complete declaration.
4. Membership omission/re-add, same-key distinct lifetimes, rekey, and full/local order updates preserve per-fact dependencies. Held references never retarget.
5. Nested scopes across scalar/aggregate connections obey the same complete-declaration validation horizon and do not share permission implicitly. Reentry, then-getter errors, failure/recovery, queued/in-flight delivery and disposal controls stay unchanged.
6. No-setter scopes, unchanged effective support, unknown declaration and application hidden-input misuse keep their established distinctions. Misuse is not a safety pass.

If this extension is not authorized, continue scalar/field and conservative aggregate qualification, but mark A0 case 5 unfulfilled. Do not claim complete A1 or silently narrow the intended product. Mechanism-level work may be reviewed separately; it cannot substitute for this source-scope decision.

## Review disposition

Independent first proposal review required explicit authored-input versus outgoing-projection completeness, exact receipt supersession, and separate owned-versus-surviving-copy rejection witnesses. Those clarifications above address all three. The first proposal is preserved separately. This revised proposal remains a request for owner selection, not a selected API or new passing test contract.
