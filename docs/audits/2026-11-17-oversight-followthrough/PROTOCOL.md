## Hard outcomes and negative controls

- No unauthorized effect; valid approved command succeeds.
- Same exact command after lost acknowledgment returns the original receipt with
  one database effect. Reusing identity for changed content is rejected.
- Precommit kill exposes no partial effect or terminal receipt.
- Stop/revoke prevents new work; possible past effects remain uncertain until
  reconciled. A new session never silently adopts an old pending native owner.
- Out-of-order receipts settle only their original owners. Human edits survive.
- Monitoring loss cannot alter settlement. Count/byte overflow and disconnected
  intervals are visible; a current snapshot cannot imply complete history.
- Fake secrets in tokens, free text and errors do not enter the redacted
  investigation/export channel. Authorized review of exact business changes is
  a separately scoped surface.
- Planted bypasses of authorization, deduplication, entity lifetime, native
  settlement and gap reporting fail their named positive-case oracles.
