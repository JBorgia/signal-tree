# Findings and hypotheses register

Updated November 18, 2026. This register consolidates the completed study and its
interpretation; it does not generate new runtime measurements or replace the
source identities, commands and controls in the linked reports. Selected sources
remain v15 `e45fd69616586cae6f7f9e8622c082199d0fded0` and v16
`f8cf26e50193ca8aa5580da15b739129313f256e`. The study branch has a different runtime.

**Measured** means an identified executed workload, not a universal property.
**Source-supported** means an inspected mechanism without quantified attribution.
**Conditional model** means arithmetic on stated assumptions, not observed demand.
**Hypothesis** requires a discriminating test. **Judgment** is a recommendation,
not an experimental result. Rejected premises and adverse observations remain here.

## Established findings

| ID | Finding and status | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F01 | Both lines reduce unused allocation automatically. Measured and source-supported. | Absent revisions already mean zero; new subjects clear old entries. Ordinary observation records wait for a consuming claim while writer interception and claimed identity remain. Position IDs were already lazy. Sparse isolated savings disappear with dense demand; see [candidates](CANDIDATES.md), [cost map](COST-MAP.md) and [verification](VERIFICATION.md). No new public configuration was selected. |
| F02 | V16 aligned fresh population avoids redundant preparation. Measured. | Ordinary placement can reuse completed physical placement without dropping detached values, facts, validation or continuity. Production Angular factory medians at 10,000 rows fall from 170.9 to 133.0 ms and at 50,000 from 840.3 to 638.7 ms. Every larger-store pair improves beyond observed A/A variation; this is not a confidence interval or universal speedup. [Application comparison](APPLICATION-COMPARISON.md). |
| F03 | The ordering investigation repaired an existing v16 Link defect and a subsequent work-bound regression. Executed counterexamples. | Prepending x,y to a,b previously gave different physical and Link order. Final eligible adjacency fixes delivery; stable heads/cursors remove the fallback scan caught by six unchanged rollback bounds. 587,500 generated cases and focused checks are bounded evidence, not proof for all graphs or all rollback work. [Candidates](CANDIDATES.md), [verification](VERIFICATION.md). |
| F04 | V15's strongest current benefit is compatible sparse memory reduction. Measured. | At 50,000 rows, whole-app populated heap falls by a paired median 917,956 bytes; small factory changes are mixed/noise-scale. The app's selected factory is 40.8 ms. Do not add old retention gains to this change or label it universally faster. [Application comparison](APPLICATION-COMPARISON.md). |
| F05 | V16 still has a substantial ordinary startup premium. Measured. | At 50,000 rows, selected v15/v16 factories are 40.8/638.7 ms and populated app heap 26.946/31.338 MB. At 500 rows they are 4.2/13.8 ms. Heap includes Angular and the local service, not just the library or total browser RAM. Application sizes are workload tiers, not sampled industries or devices. [Application comparison](APPLICATION-COMPARISON.md). |
| F06 | The common application shows comparable steady checked work for several implementations, with a slower NgRx settlement path. Measured plus source-supported mechanism. | At 50,000 rows native/v15/v16/TanStack achieve roughly 211–224 checked commands/s; NgRx achieves 89.6 in this implementation. NgRx authoritative entity updates copy the entity map and ID array, but their exact CPU share is not profiled. Its narrow optimistic latency is good and excludes later responses. The closed-loop harness waits for settlement before the next synthetic human; no independent-arrival or INP conclusion. [Application comparison](APPLICATION-COMPARISON.md). |
| F07 | Startup gains do not establish lower active CPU or a lifetime crossover. Measured adverse and mixed results. | Full native v16 active-Link/owner workloads show slower 1,000-row rollback and synchronous 10,000-row confirmation in all three pairs. Other phases and A/A variation are mixed. The common app's 10,000-row settlement also grows by paired median 1.1 ms. Do not erase these observations with the startup improvement. [Native comparison](NATIVE-COMPARISON.md), [application comparison](APPLICATION-COMPARISON.md). |
| F08 | Deferred representation savings are demand-dependent. Measured. | Fully claiming/rekeying consumes sparse savings. An identified v16 dense scalar rearm cohort increases by paired median 270,424 bytes; final dense revisions increase by 1,600 bytes, all three positive. Different engines, artifacts and workloads do not supply a universal per-object byte price. [Candidates](CANDIDATES.md), [cost map](COST-MAP.md). |
| F09 | Tree collection and retained-history cost are separate. Measured. | All twelve recorded native heap trees collect after owner return; positive whole-process residual differences remain. Retired payload backings/candidates reach zero, while exact retained facts can grow through fresh lifetimes. One heap pair per profile and bounded cycles do not prove universal absence of leaks, zero residuals or bounded history. [Native comparison](NATIVE-COMPARISON.md). |
| F10 | Opt-in use is not identical to zero built-in cost. Source-supported and measured where cited. | Transactions, restoration, Links and recording are selected uses, but shared machinery remains. Unbundled barrel evaluation can register Link factories; bundlers can omit unused modules. Current publication activation is automatic. No Link does not imply no publication cost. Diagnostic CPU profiles are single-run attribution leads, not comparable phase speedups; first-use work is part of the cost. [Cost map](COST-MAP.md). |
| F11 | V15 and v16 oversight passes assert different contracts. Executed. | The v15 refusal witness retains pending work but releases consequences; v16's checked refusal retains pending work and withholds them until recovery. Common green checks do not mean equivalent native containment. The in-memory durability counterexample remains negative. [Verification](VERIFICATION.md). |
| F12 | A v16 application can combine native coordination with durable backend enforcement. Executed integration, not general certification. | The installed application checks concurrent approvals, binding, session isolation and restart after effect-before-receipt failure. A new native owner reconciles with the same SQLite authority. This is not a persisted frontend owner, arbitrary remote exactly-once execution, production IdP validation, real-model quality or human comprehension evidence. Recording is bounded and makes gaps visible. [Verification](VERIFICATION.md), [oversight follow-through](../2026-11-17-oversight-followthrough/README.md). |
| F13 | Cross-version reuse is selective. Measured/adopted versus parked. | Zero revisions and first-claim records transfer to both lines. Earlier retention fixes were already shared. V15 already passes the ordered-prepend witnesses; v16's resolver and population replay are not generic v15 optimizations. Empty-plan/lazy-container adaptation and v15 live-Link bulk egress remain unmeasured leads. [Transfer report](CROSS-VERSION.md). |
| F14 | Qualification is candidate-specific and includes explicit numerical failures. Verified. | V15 covers 90/91 normal gates through the preserved correction; v16 86/87. All respective sensitivity proofs complete. V15's strict demo and 146 browser checks pass; v16 has 137 behavior passes plus a size failure and strict-build failure. Specific cost acceptance does not change ceilings or make failing predicates green. [Verification](VERIFICATION.md), [review](REVIEW.md). |
| F15 | Public runtime revenue and private product economics are distinct. Conditional model. | Public license revenue is zero. Time recovery, realization, prices, support, churn and acquisition assumptions generate the results. Base payroll cash, avoided-risk cash and performance cash savings are zero. Base v15 migration cases are negative over three years; bounded new modules fare better. The 10,000-account base supplier case is positive but heavy support/CAC reverses it. No observed willingness to pay, TAM, earnings forecast or sector-profitability shortcut. [Economics](ECONOMICS.md). |
| F16 | Evidence integrity does not establish every scientific or production claim. Verified scope. | The frozen portable archive verifies 22,421 payloads, isolated read-denial checks and negative controls. Earlier artifact identities and unavailable preliminary generator bytes stay disclosed. New documentation and the dated delivery observation below are outside that archive; they do not relabel it. [Evidence parts](EVIDENCE-PARTS.json), [verification](VERIFICATION.md). |

## Rejected claims and invalid premises

| ID | Disposition | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | Do not remove evidence merely because there is no current observer. Source-only rejection, not an executed prototype. | Late readers/owners can create obligations that still need that evidence. No concrete equivalent replacement was established. |
| R02 | Do not promise stronger caller-input deep immutability. Invalid comparison premise. | The stronger probe also fails the frozen baseline; it is not evidence of a newly introduced regression. |
| R03 | Do not adopt the tested v15 EntityMap method split. Executed rejection. | The particular split increases delivered gzip size. This does not prove every future decomposition is worse. |
| R04 | Do not equate fewer allocations with lower CPU everywhere. Disproved generalization. | Dense demand pays deferred costs, active native CPU has adverse cases, and exact history can grow after payload retirement. |
| R05 | Do not call v16 an automatic AI safety, authorization or durable audit layer. Unsupported product claim. | External authority, durable effects, identity, display, retention and model quality require separate application/backend work. |
| R06 | Do not claim latest-library superiority from the pinned cohort. Unsupported extrapolation. | The study uses TanStack DB 0.15.2/Angular DB 0.2.12. TanStack's October 29, 2026 RC announcement describes newer APIs; those were not measured here. See the current-source notes below. |

R01–R03 retain their raw rationale and earlier evidence in the
[candidate ledger](CANDIDATES.md) and [transfer report](CROSS-VERSION.md).

## Open hypotheses and discriminating evidence

These are research leads, not an automatically authorized implementation queue.
Before another experiment, select a concrete decision, matched contract and
cheapest useful falsifier. Failed hypotheses should stay recorded.

| ID | Hypothesis/status | Evidence needed to support or falsify it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | Most users of small ordinary forms would not perceive the state-library difference. Untested user-experience inference. | Matched complete screens, controlled and real-user cold/warm timings, field interaction-to-next-paint and blinded task observations. A repeatable perceptual/task difference would falsify the broad inference. Sub-20-ms store construction alone does not prove invisibility. |
| H02 | V16 large eager stores can produce a noticeable cold-start delay when construction is on the critical main-thread path. Supported mechanism; perceptual magnitude unmeasured. | Trace construction through first useful paint/input on actual screens. Compare actual resident-data loading strategies without changing ownership semantics. Virtualizing DOM rows does not remove already-constructed state. Hidden/off-critical-path construction may remove the visible difference. |
| H03 | Lower-resource mobile devices amplify startup, download and memory costs. Plausible, unmeasured here. | Physical representative Android/iOS devices, cold/warm load, production network/cache conditions, resident rows, first useful paint, field/controlled INP, long tasks, GC and sustained interaction. No desktop-to-phone multiplier, battery saving or tab-reload threshold can be inferred from these heap rows. |
| H04 | An all-day application's reduced repeated work repays construction cost. Workload-dependent, not established generally. | Measure the same full native semantics over a representative lifetime, including startup, first use, independent event arrivals, settling, recovery, idle and teardown. Current adverse native CPU cases can prevent a crossover. |
| H05 | V15 identity/restoration/coordination reduce application code and defects enough to repay their cost. Product hypothesis. | Matched competent implementations with required behavior, developer/review time, maintenance changes, hidden overlap/undo/retirement tests and field defects. Count app code and tests avoided, not just library APIs. Native/NgRx can implement the same visible behavior. |
| H06 | V16's explicit pending/refusal/recovery state improves human oversight. Functional foundation demonstrated; human benefit untested. | Real investigator/user tasks with causal mistakes, uncertainty and evidence gaps; task accuracy, false confidence, recovery success and time. More displayed events or passing DOM assertions alone are insufficient. |
| H07 | Increasing AI use creates sufficient paid demand for oversight tooling. Commercial hypothesis. | Qualified buyers with concrete workflows, current alternatives, actual incidents, constrained trials, paid commitments, renewal/support burden and realized value. Sector margins, incident headlines and hypothetical price ceilings are not willingness to pay. AI may also cheapen the alternative implementation. |
| H08 | Version-pinned optimal AI guidance makes v15/v16 adoption easier. Plausible, unmeasured. | Equal agents/models/budgets and best available guidance for every library; executable starter examples, installed types and hidden behavior tests. Measure first correct completion, corrections, tokens/cost and maintainer review. Include teardown, overlapping writes, replacement, refusal and durable-effect recovery. Do not use prose length or first compile as success. |
| H09 | V15 empty-plan/lazy-container techniques can further reduce v16 cost safely. Source lead only. | Adapt witness/cancellation paths and compare sparse/dense/first-use cost while keeping late readers, reentry, retirement and point-work controls. Any cost shifting or semantic failure counts against the proposal. |
| H10 | The v15 live-Link bulk-egress scan is worth optimizing. Source lead only. | Attribute real bulk workloads before editing; quantify frequency, asymptotic work, wall CPU and allocation with equivalent ordered publication. It is not an established cause of current ordinary application startup. |
| H11 | More public configuration or package separation pays for itself. Unproven general proposal. | Demonstrate a meaningful shipped/active cost reduction in real bundles with preserved stable types/semantics, then account for combinations, first-use spikes, documentation and support. Automatic deferral is already selected where safe. |

## Practical interpretation and current primary sources

The [user-experience narrative](USER-EXPERIENCE.md) translates these results into
screens, mobile/desktop tradeoffs, new-application choices and AI-assisted adoption.
Its recommendations are product judgments, not additional benchmark outcomes.

Primary sources checked November 18, 2026, separately from frozen comparison
inputs:

- [Angular signals](https://angular.dev/guide/signals): native granular state and
  derived reads; suitable primitives do not by themselves define application
  authorization, conflict handling or durable effects.
- [NgRx SignalStore](https://ngrx.io/guide/signals/signal-store): composable Angular
  stores; do not imply that NgRx lacks entities, nested state or custom features.
- [TanStack DB overview](https://tanstack.com/db/latest/docs/overview) and
  [October 29 RC announcement](https://tanstack.com/blog/tanstack-db-1.0-rc1-going-all-in-on-oracles):
  typed collections, live relational queries and optimistic server reconciliation.
  Current documentation is recommendation context, not a performance rerun.
- [Interaction to Next Paint](https://web.dev/articles/inp): user input through
  next paint, assessed across visits and device classes. Store factory time and
  the synthetic enqueue-to-layout measure are not INP.
- [Angular AI guidance](https://angular.dev/ai/develop-with-ai): version-current
  instructions and tool integration are supported practices. It does not measure
  SignalTree adoption or establish that optimal instructions eliminate errors.
