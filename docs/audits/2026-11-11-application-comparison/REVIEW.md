# Independent review record

November 11, 2026. Reviews were scoped to the actual source and raw evidence.
They were not votes on a library-wide winner, new acceptance budget or v16
publication. Production runtime and architectural writes remained centralized.

## Measurement framing and implementation

The registered protocol distinguishes equal observable tasks from equal internal
work, native capability probes from completed application behavior, synthetic
layout latency from INP, and whole-application heap from library retention.
The common fixture supplies domain/UI/backend checks, not the competing arms'
rollback or publication implementation. Artifact identities and complete
conformance precede comparison.

Independent source review corrected alternating pair order, required dispatch
qualification whenever a variant changes dispatch, rejected model/dependency
drift and checked actual browser process identities. Deliberate fixture defects
must activate and fail at their expected stage; empty or unstarted work cannot
satisfy a healthy control. The saved helper-control receipts retain those checks.

## Raw comparison interpretation

Independent inspection checked all 87 inventory and 174 dispatch baseline
children against their schedules, aggregates and identities. The completed
offline analyzer recomputed distributions and paired values without pooling
operation samples across children. Five-child distributions and A/A controls
remain descriptive shared-host evidence.

Separate reviews checked all 42 paired inventory and 84 paired dispatch children.
Dispatch review recomputed each repeated-stage p95 and every reported paired
metric. It confirmed that the dispatch child body was unchanged and only the
private dispatch arm changed in that build's dependency closure.

The principal interpretation corrections were retained:

- Faster filtering also made 10,000-row edits slower. It is not an unconditional
  improvement.
- The original dispatch memory child never started a publication. Its near-equal
  heap could not certify populated payload-map lifetime.
- Startup and small interaction differences did not justify broad rankings.
- Native probes did not establish server-refetch or full-product semantics that
  their wrappers never exercised.

## Lifecycle evidence

Independent raw review verified all 24 longer-lifecycle children and 408 stage
files against their hashes. Every child passed. Original inventory values
sampled at the first hundred edits stayed live until teardown in both arms;
later replacements collected. The pinned local-only sync closure retains initial
data, but that source observation is not a captured heap retaining path.
The variant's larger 10,000-row live heap remains disclosed.

Publication review confirmed exact domain envelopes, public/transport equality,
historical digests and actual started/acknowledged/dismissed states. Public
payload roots collected after dismissal in both arms. The count of observed
payload identities is not an allocation count.

The reviewers identified an untested nonempty-map destruction boundary. The
additional started-publication teardown witness then passed twelve fresh
children. Independent review checked all 120 stage files, unique PIDs, schedule,
eight live/current payloads before destruction, exact late-response counts and
every paired heap value. Candidate public payload roots collected while the old
backend remained held; baseline roots collected after its release. No ninth
publication or UI resurrection occurred. A late acknowledgement was not delivered.

No concrete remaining correctness or measurement blocker was demonstrated for
these bounded application findings. The reviews do not establish unlimited
boundedness, complete callback coverage, library-only allocation costs or a new
runtime qualification.

## Report and packaging corrections

A separate review recomputed the report's comparative and lifecycle numbers and
checked the copied summaries against their original packets. One three-decimal
heap rounding error was corrected to 8.092 MiB. The wording now distinguishes
post-edit checkpoints from the initial checkpoint before weak tracking.

Generated Python bytecode was accidentally staged and was rejected by the
source-control gate. It was removed from the index and the tool directory now
ignores interpreter caches. The gate was rerun without changing its rules.
The initial failed result is retained.

Archive preparation recovered historical source versions only by matching their
recorded hashes. This includes a preserved v15.4.7 manifest, an exact dispatch
child excerpt and a reconstructed intermediate native-probe source that matches
its original digest. Synthetic invalid fixtures remain explicitly opaque;
accepted evidence bindings remain parsed and checked. Authentication screenshots
and interpreter caches are excluded. The final archive verification receipt
records the actual create, pack, extracted-copy verification and corruption
checks; those operations are distinct from this source-review record.

Full-size archive verification exposed repeated decompression when revisiting
payloads inside gzip. The reader now decompresses once into a seekable temporary
tar before applying the same member, checksum and reference-closure checks.
Synthetic original-free verification, tamper/missing-payload rejection and unsafe
path/symlink rejection pass after the change. The first complete archive and its
slow verification receipts are retained separately from the final archive.
